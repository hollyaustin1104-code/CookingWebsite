When Conversions Go Wrong in the Kitchen

Cooking conversions can be tricky! Using the wrong type of measurement can completely change your dish. Here's a famous example from Najie Mercedes on Spring Baking Championship Season 2, Episode 1.

Najie needed 3 cups of sugar for her 'naked' cake. She accidentally used 21 ounces by weight instead of 24 fluid ounces by volume. Because of this, her cake was not sweet enough, and the judges even said it tasted like bread!

To prevent mistakes like this, understanding the difference between weight and volume is essential.
