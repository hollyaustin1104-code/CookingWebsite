From Najie's View

Hi, I’m Najie! I want to share what happened during my baking challenge and what I learned.

When I was making my 'naked' cake, the recipe called for 3 cups of sugar. I accidentally used 21 ounces by weight instead of 24 fluid ounces by volume. I didn’t realize the difference between weight ounces and fluid ounces, and my cake ended up less sweet than it should have been.

The judges were honest but tough – they said my cake tasted like bread! Lesson learned: always check if a recipe is asking for weight or volume.

Next time, I’ll double-check conversions before mixing ingredients. Mistakes happen, but they’re great for learning!
